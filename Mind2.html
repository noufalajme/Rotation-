Ministry of Investment

Development Mind Map

Internal
The department improves internal processes and develops systems based on departmental needs, whether by enhancing existing solutions or building new ones.
ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning):
Used to manage all core internal operations of the ministry, including:
- HR: Payroll, Org Structure, People Profile, and Travel Management
- Finance: Accounts Payable (AP), Accounts Receivable (AR), Budget Control
- Material Management (MM): Vendor Management, RFP, Bidding, Contracting & Payments

SuccessFactors:
- PM/GM: Tracks performance goals and evaluations
- Learning Management: Manages employee training
- Succession Planning: Checks readiness for promotion
- ICPI: Links ERP, SuccessFactors, and FileNet for smooth data flow

SharePoint:
- Internal platform for employee collaboration
- Key feature: ShareDrive for file sharing
- Most used services: Overtime, Travel, and Leaves

FileNet:
- IBM product used to manage official documents
- Implemented the new system “Moamalaat” for handling government correspondence and internal transactions
The Development Department uses SAP UI5 to build modern and user-friendly web interfaces for systems like SAP ERP and SuccessFactors. It helps improve how employees interact with these systems by making the screens easier and more efficient to use.
The department uses two main methodologies: Waterfall and Scrum.

Waterfall: A step-by-step method where each phase is finished before starting the next.

Scrum: A flexible method where the team works in short cycles (called sprints), meets regularly, and delivers work in small parts.
External
In most countries, there are organizations called Investment Promotion Organizations (IPO). These entities are responsible for marketing the country’s investment opportunities to attract both local and international investors.

In Saudi Arabia, this important role is carried out by the Ministry of Investment through its Business Development Department. The department works to professionally market opportunities within the Kingdom and connect them with foreign investors.
To reach investors effectively and in a modern way, the Ministry builds electronic portals that serve two main functions:
- Marketing investment opportunities
- Providing e-services, such as submitting and tracking investment applications
Investment applications received through the portals are transferred to the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system, which manages three main areas:
- Marketing: Tracks promotional campaigns
- Sales: Follows up on deals and ongoing investment opportunities
- Service: Supports investors after submitting applications
The Ministry sends inspectors to visit investor sites and check that everything is being done according to Saudi laws and official regulations.
- The department uses BCM (Business Communication Management) to communicate with investors through one unified channel (Only Channel).
- The current platform is Hybris, with future plans to move to WordPress.
- Integration tools used include:
-- UData
-- CPI

Data and transactions are shared between CRM and C4C, then stored in the Business Warehouse.
Microsoft Power BI is used to display performance reports and analysis in the Presentation Layer.
